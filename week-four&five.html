SLAVE TO THE ALGORITHM

In this activity, for 24 hours, I based my decision making/activities purely on chance/luck/the roll of a die. This tested my control on what I would do that day, which I normally take for granted.

¤ Breakfast: sweet (odd) or savoury (even) rolled even, so savoury breakfast!
¤ Hair down (even) or down (up)? rolled odd, so hair up!
¤ Walk to class (odd) or tram to class (even)? rolled even, so tram to class!
¤ Matcha (odd) or no matcha (even)? rolled odd, so matcha!
¤ Eat dinner at home (odd) or go out (even)? rolled odd, so dinner at home!

The rule: roll the dice - outcome is odd or even, there are two choices that i can choose from in the activities i do
