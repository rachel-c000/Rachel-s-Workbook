Week 2

We were introduced to this free, web-based 3D design modeling tool which instructs on how to build the correct circuits through diagrams. This was a really useful tool in helping us understand what each element is and looks like, and how they connect to each other.

The Machine

We learnt the power of the Arduino! Before this class, I have never heard or seen of Ardunio's before but I thought it had a pretty cool name haha. I had seen some physical computing projects on the internet such as pocket computers, but I had never tried or researched about them before.

WoTs IN THE BOX?

Lots of components!

This was a very fun and interesting activity, it made me feel like I was in primary school all over again. We each in pairs had a box of these components and we had to lay them all out on a brown peice of paper, separate them from one another, and based off of just looks and current knowledge, what we think each part is called.

Blink and buzz

HUNTER AND GATHERER

Angela Bulloch

Her approach to abstract but captivating conceptual art is interesting as the simple shapes but bold colours works well. How she represents different colours through different boxes is reflective in a sense which peaked my interest and adds to the definition of interactive media.

Camille Utterback

Her installations focuses on linking computational systems through software programming to interactive with these human movement gestures. Her most famous work is, Text Rain, where is features words, letters and phrases from a poem by Evan Zimroth. “Talk, You”. It projects the letters and words falling like objects from the sky where is momentarily rests on the heads, arms and shoulders of participants viewing the work. I found the concept of this interaction with her installation so interesting and alluring the way it reacts to the audience around it.
