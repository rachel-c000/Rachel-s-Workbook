workbook

a documentation of process, ideas & making





ideation

Some ideations in class with physical prototyping ideas using materials given in the studio. This was an idea I came up with — a beanie the size of a globe with different buttons which when clicked open previews of webpages.

inspiration

When going on Pinterest to find inspiration, I came across a lot of photography shots of market stalls and that sparked my interest. This would later influence the direction of my workbook.

sketching

Following the inspiration images plus other market stall references and my imagination, I developed some sketches for the workbook.

prompting

In class, there was a newly released platform from Google called Stich. Stich is a platform that can ai generate ux/ui prototypes based off prompts. This was scary news to the class, but once we started generating, a group consensus was that it was not great.

building

Therefore, taking it into my own hands, I started making my own graphics. This was first done in figma where I made the shapes and colours for my stall, then I imported them onto Canva to use their style functions.

the stall

This is the output from Canva that I got.

Into VS Code

I then started coding it into html and css on vs code.

Star Theme

At this stage, this was after the Workbook Part A submission, I really wanted to go out-of-the-box and not play it safe. I'm really into stars and putting them in my everyday life and graphics in general, so I tried to move the stall to outer space and have the items be stars instead of real fruits and vegetables. However, this was a struggle in aesthethics, colours and everything else above.

More Stars

I kept going with this star theme and extended the scope of my homepage to be something grander and bigger than my Workbook A submission, I wanted the user to move across the page and visit different stalls and places that a market would usually have.

Back to Original

I eventually found it too hard to keep up with the star aesethetic, and while it looked good in my head, I couldn't achieve it how I wanted to, so I went back to the original aesthetics and found things flowed much better after that.
